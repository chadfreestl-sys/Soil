Scientific boundary: These are relative activity indices (0–100), not organism counts, laboratory measurements, or calibrated predictions. Adjusting a slider illustrates model assumptions, not a guaranteed treatment outcome.

What the model is illustrating

In actual soil, water and oxygen are coupled through pore structure and drainage. These controls are deliberately independent to let learners isolate each variable; not every combination is physically realistic.

Questions to investigate

What changes when moisture increases but oxygen remains low? How does organic input affect modeled bacteria and fungi? Why might a compacted, saturated root zone respond differently from a well-drained one?
